Title: Hives
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/hives.html
Language: English
Also called: Urticaria
Health topic groups: Skin, Hair and Nails, Immune System, Symptoms

Summary:
Hives are red and sometimes itchy bumps on your skin. An allergic reaction to a drug or food usually
causes them. Allergic reactions cause your body to release chemicals that can make your skin swell
up in hives. People who have other allergies are more likely to get hives than other people. Other
causes include infections and stress. Hives are very common. They usually go away on their own, but
if you have a serious case, you might need medicine or a shot. In rare cases, hives can cause a
dangerous swelling in your airways, making it hard to breathe - which is a medical emergency.
