Title: Foreign Bodies
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/foreignbodies.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Injuries and Wounds

Summary:
If you've ever gotten a splinter or had sand in your eye, you've had experience with a foreign body.
A foreign body is something that is stuck inside you but isn't supposed to be there. You may inhale
or swallow a foreign body, or you may get one from an injury to almost any part of your body.
Foreign bodies are more common in small children, who sometimes stick things in their mouths, ears,
and noses. Some foreign bodies, like a small splinter, do not cause serious harm. Inhaled or
swallowed foreign bodies may cause choking or bowel obstruction and may require medical care.
