Title: Medicaid
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/medicaid.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Health System

Summary:
Medicaid is government health insurance that helps many low-income people in the United States to
pay their medical bills . The Federal government sets up general guidelines for the program, but
each state has its own rules. Your state might require you to pay a part of the cost for some
medical services. You have to meet certain requirements to get Medicaid help. These might involve:
Your age Whether you are pregnant, blind, or have a disability Your income and resources Whether or
not you are a U.S. citizen or, if not, what your immigration status is Centers for Medicare and
Medicaid Services
