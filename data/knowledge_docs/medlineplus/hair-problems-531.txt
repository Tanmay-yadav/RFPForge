Title: Hair Problems
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/hairproblems.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Skin, Hair and Nails

Summary:
The average person has 5 million hairs. Hair grows all over your body except on your lips, palms,
and the soles of your feet. It takes about a month for healthy hair to grow half an inch. Most hairs
grow for up to six years and then fall out. New hairs grow in their place. Hair helps keep you warm.
It also protects your eyes, ears and nose from small particles in the air. Common problem with the
hair and scalp include hair loss , infections, and flaking .
