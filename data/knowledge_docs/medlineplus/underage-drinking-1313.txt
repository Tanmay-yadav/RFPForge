Title: Underage Drinking
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/underagedrinking.html
Language: English
Also called: Teenage drinking
Health topic groups: Children and Teenagers, Substance Use and Disorders

Summary:
Alcohol is the most widely misused substance among America's youth. Drinking by young people has big
health and safety risks. It is dangerous because it: Causes many deaths and injuries Can lead to
poor decisions about engaging in risky behavior, such as drinking and driving or unprotected sex
Increases the risk of physical and sexual assault Can lead to other problems, such as trouble in
school May interfere with brain development Increases the risk of alcohol problems later in life
Kids often begin drinking to look "cool" or fit in with their peers. Parents can help their kids
avoid alcohol problems. Open communication and conversations about drinking are important. So is
being involved in your child's life. Get help for your child if you suspect a drinking problem. NIH:
National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and Alcoholism
