Title: Health Screening
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/healthscreening.html
Language: English
Also called: Screening tests
Health topic groups: Wellness and Lifestyle

Summary:
Screenings are tests that look for diseases before you have symptoms. Screening tests can find
diseases early, when they're easier to treat. You can get some screenings in your doctor's office.
Others need special equipment, so you may need to go to a different office or clinic. Some
conditions that doctors commonly screen for include: Breast cancer and cervical cancer in women
Colorectal cancer Diabetes High blood pressure High cholesterol Osteoporosis Overweight and obesity
Prostate cancer in men Which tests you need depends on your age, your sex, your family history, and
whether you have risk factors for certain diseases. After a screening test, ask when you will get
the results and whom to talk to about them. Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality
