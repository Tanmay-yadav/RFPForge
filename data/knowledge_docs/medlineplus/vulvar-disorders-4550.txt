Title: Vulvar Disorders
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/vulvardisorders.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Women, Female Reproductive System

Summary:
The vulva is the external part of a woman's genitals. Some problems you can have with the vulvar
area include: Vaginitis or vulvovaginitis, swelling or infection of the vulva and vagina Skin
problems due to allergy Vulvar cancer Vulvodynia, or vulvar pain Symptoms may include redness,
itching, pain, or cracks in the skin. Treatment depends on the cause.
