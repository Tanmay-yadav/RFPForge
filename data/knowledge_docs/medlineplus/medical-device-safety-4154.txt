Title: Medical Device Safety
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/medicaldevicesafety.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Safety Issues

Summary:
A medical device is any product used to diagnose, cure, or treat a condition, or to prevent disease.
They range from small and simple, like a blood glucose meter, to large and complicated, like a
ventilator. You might use one at home or at work, or you may need one in a hospital. To use medical
devices safely: Know how your device works. Keep instructions close by Understand and properly
respond to device alarms Have a back-up plan and supplies in the event of an emergency Keep
emergency numbers available and update them as needed Educate your family and caregivers about your
device Food and Drug Administration

Safety note: This file is for educational retrieval only and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified clinician.
