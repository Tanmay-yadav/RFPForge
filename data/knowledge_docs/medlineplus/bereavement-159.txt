Title: Bereavement
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/bereavement.html
Language: English
Also called: Grief
Health topic groups: Mental Health and Behavior, Social/Family Issues

Summary:
Bereavement is the period of grief and mourning after a death. When you grieve, it's part of the
normal process of reacting to a loss. You may experience grief as a mental, physical, social or
emotional reaction. Mental reactions can include anger, guilt, anxiety, sadness and despair.
Physical reactions can include sleeping problems, changes in appetite, physical problems or illness.
How long bereavement lasts can depend on how close you were to the person who died, if the person's
death was expected and other factors. Friends, family and faith may be sources of support. Grief
counseling or grief therapy is also helpful to some people. NIH: National Cancer Institute
