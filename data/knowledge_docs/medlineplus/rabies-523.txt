Title: Rabies
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/rabies.html
Language: English
Also called: Hydrophobia
Health topic groups: Infections

Summary:
Rabies is a deadly animal disease caused by a virus. It can happen in wild animals, including
raccoons, skunks, bats and foxes, or in dogs, cats or farm animals. People get it from the bite of
an infected animal. In people, symptoms of rabies include fever, headache and fatigue, then
confusion, hallucinations and paralysis. Once the symptoms begin, the disease is usually fatal. A
series of shots can prevent rabies in people exposed to the virus. You need to get them right away.
If an animal bites you, wash the wound well; then get medical care. To help prevent rabies:
Vaccinate your pet. Rabies vaccines are available for dogs, cats and farm animals Don't let pets
roam Don't approach stray animals. Animals with rabies might be aggressive and vicious, or tired and
weak Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
