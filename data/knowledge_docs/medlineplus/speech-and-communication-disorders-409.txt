Title: Speech and Communication Disorders
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/speechandcommunicationdisorders.html
Language: English
Also called: Communication disorders
Health topic groups: Brain and Nerves, Mouth and Teeth, Symptoms

Summary:
Many disorders can affect our ability to speak and communicate. They range from saying sounds
incorrectly to being completely unable to speak or understand speech. Causes include: Hearing
disorders and deafness Voice problems , such as dysphonia or those caused by cleft lip or palate
Speech problems like stuttering Developmental disabilities Learning disabilities Autism spectrum
disorder Brain injury Stroke Some speech and communication problems may be genetic. Often, no one
knows the causes. By first grade, about 5% of children have noticeable speech disorders. Speech and
language therapy can help. NIH: National Institute on Deafness and Other Communication Disorders
