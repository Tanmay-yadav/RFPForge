Title: Uncommon Infant and Newborn Problems
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/uncommoninfantandnewbornproblems.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Children and Teenagers

Summary:
It can be scary when your baby is sick, especially when it is not an everyday problem like a cold or
a fever. You may not know whether the problem is serious or how to treat it. If you have concerns
about your baby's health, call your health care provider right away. Learning information about your
baby's condition can help ease your worry. Do not be afraid to ask questions about your baby's care.
By working together with your health care provider, you make sure that your baby gets the best care
possible.

Safety note: This file is for educational retrieval only and does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment from a qualified clinician.
