Title: Mood Disorders
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/mooddisorders.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Mental Health and Behavior

Summary:
Most people feel sad or irritable from time to time. They may say they're in a bad mood. A mood
disorder is different. It affects a person's everyday emotional state. Nearly one in ten people aged
18 and older have mood disorders. These include depression and bipolar disorder (also called manic
depression). Mood disorders can increase a person's risk for heart disease, diabetes, and other
diseases. Treatments include medication, psychotherapy, or a combination of both. With treatment,
most people with mood disorders can lead productive lives.
