Title: Myositis
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/myositis.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Bones, Joints and Muscles

Summary:
Myositis means inflammation of the muscles that you use to move your body. An injury, infection, or
autoimmune disease can cause it. Two specific kinds are polymyositis and dermatomyositis.
Polymyositis causes muscle weakness, usually in the muscles closest to the trunk of your body.
Dermatomyositis causes muscle weakness, plus a skin rash. Other symptoms of myositis may include:
Fatigue after walking or standing Tripping or falling Trouble swallowing or breathing Doctors may
use a physical exam, lab tests, imaging tests and a muscle biopsy to diagnose myositis. There is no
cure for these diseases, but you can treat the symptoms. Polymyositis and dermatomyositis are first
treated with high doses of a corticosteroid . Other options include medications, physical therapy,
exercise, heat therapy, assistive devices, and rest. NIH: National Institute of Neurological
Disorders and Stroke
