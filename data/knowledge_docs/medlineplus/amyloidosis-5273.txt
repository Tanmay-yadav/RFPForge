Title: Amyloidosis
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/amyloidosis.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Metabolic Problems

Summary:
Amyloidosis occurs when abnormal proteins called amyloids build up and form deposits. The deposits
can collect in organs such as the kidney and heart. This can cause the organs to become stiff and
unable to work the way they should. There are three main types of amyloidosis: Primary - with no
known cause Secondary - caused by another disease, including some types of cancer Familial - passed
down through genes Symptoms can vary, depending upon which organs are affected. Treatment depends on
the type of amyloidosis you have. The goal is to help with symptoms and limit the production of
proteins. If another disease is the cause, it needs to be treated.
