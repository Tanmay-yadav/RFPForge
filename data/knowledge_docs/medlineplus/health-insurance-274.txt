Title: Health Insurance
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/healthinsurance.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Health System

Summary:
Health insurance helps protect you from high medical care costs. It is a contract between you and
your insurance company. You buy a plan or policy, and the company agrees to pay part of your
expenses when you need medical care. Many people in the United States get a health insurance policy
through their employers. In most cases, the employer helps pay for that insurance. Insurance through
employers is often with a managed care plan. These plans contract with health care providers and
medical facilities to provide care for members at reduced costs. You can also purchase health
insurance on your own. People who meet certain requirements can qualify for government health
insurance, such as Medicare and Medicaid . The Affordable Care Act expands health insurance coverage
for many people in the U.S.
