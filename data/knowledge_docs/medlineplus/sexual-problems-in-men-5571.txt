Title: Sexual Problems in Men
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/sexualproblemsinmen.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Men, Sexual Health Issues, Male Reproductive System

Summary:
Many men have sexual problems. They become more common as men age. Problems can include: Erectile
dysfunction Reduced or lost interest in sex Problems with ejaculation Low testosterone Stress,
illness, medicines, or emotional problems may also be factors. Occasional problems with sexual
function are common. If problems last more than a few months or cause distress for you or your
partner, you should see your health care provider.
