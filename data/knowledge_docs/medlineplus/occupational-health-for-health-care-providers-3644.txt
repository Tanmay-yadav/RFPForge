Title: Occupational Health for Health Care Providers
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/occupationalhealthforhealthcareproviders.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Safety Issues, Health System

Summary:
Health care workers are exposed to many job hazards. These can include : Infections Needle injuries
Back injuries Allergy-causing substances Violence Stress Follow good job safety and injury
prevention practices. They can reduce your risk of health problems. Use protective equipment, follow
infection control guidelines, learn the right way to lift heavy objects, and find ways to manage
stress. National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health
