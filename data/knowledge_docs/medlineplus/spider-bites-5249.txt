Title: Spider Bites
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/spiderbites.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Injuries and Wounds

Summary:
Though many people are afraid of spiders, they rarely bite people unless threatened. Most spider
bites are harmless. Occasionally, spider bites can cause allergic reactions. And bites by the
venomous black widow and brown recluse spiders can be very dangerous to people. If you are bitten by
a spider, you may see a reaction similar to that of a bee sting, including redness, pain and
swelling at the site. To treat a spider bite: Wash the area well with soap and water Apply an ice
pack or a wet compress to the area Take over-the-counter pain medicine, if needed Consider using
antihistamines for severe swelling Seek medical treatment for small children and adults with severe
symptoms
