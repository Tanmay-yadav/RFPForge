Title: Dental Health
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/dentalhealth.html
Language: English
Also called: Oral health
Health topic groups: Mouth and Teeth, Wellness and Lifestyle

Summary:
It's important to take care of your mouth and teeth starting in childhood . If you don't, you could
have problems with your teeth and gums - like cavities or even tooth loss. Here's how to keep your
mouth and teeth healthy: Brush your teeth twice a day with a fluoride toothpaste Clean between your
teeth every day with floss or another type of between-the-teeth cleaner Snack smart - limit sugary
snacks Don't smoke or chew tobacco See your dentist or oral health professional regularly NIH:
National Institute of Dental and Craniofacial Research
