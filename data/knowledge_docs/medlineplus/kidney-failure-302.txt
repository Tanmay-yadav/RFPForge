Title: Kidney Failure
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/kidneyfailure.html
Language: English
Also called: ESRD, End-stage renal disease, Renal failure
Health topic groups: Kidneys and Urinary System

Summary:
Healthy kidneys clean your blood by removing excess fluid, minerals, and wastes. They also make
hormones that keep your bones strong and your blood healthy. But if the kidneys are damaged, they
don't work properly. Harmful wastes can build up in your body. Your blood pressure may rise. Your
body may retain excess fluid and not make enough red blood cells. This is called kidney failure. If
your kidneys fail, you need treatment to replace the work they normally do. The treatment options
are dialysis or a kidney transplant . Each treatment has benefits and drawbacks. No matter which
treatment you choose, you'll need to make some changes in your life, including how you eat and plan
your activities. But with the help of health care providers, family, and friends, most people with
kidney failure can lead full and active lives. NIH: National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and
Kidney Diseases
