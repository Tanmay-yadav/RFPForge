Title: Organ Donation
Source: MedlinePlus
URL: https://medlineplus.gov/organdonation.html
Language: English
Health topic groups: Transplantation and Donation

Summary:
Organ donation takes healthy organs and tissues from one person for transplantation into another.
Experts say that the organs from one donor can save or help as many as 50 people. Organs you can
donate include: Internal organs: Kidneys, heart, liver, pancreas, intestines, lungs Skin Bone and
bone marrow Cornea Most organ and tissue donations occur after the donor has died. But some organs
and tissues can be donated while the donor is alive. People of all ages and background can be organ
donors. If you are under age 18, your parent or guardian must give you permission to become a donor.
If you are 18 or older you can show you want to be a donor by signing a donor card. You should also
let your family know your wishes. Health Resources and Services Administration
